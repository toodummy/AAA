# STORMZILLA — Steam store page copy

**Short description (≤300 chars)**
You are the tornado. Start as a dust devil sucking up chickens and grow into a city-eating F6 monster. Rip up cows, cars, houses and skyscrapers, mutate into a Firenado or Sharknado, and survive the army's increasingly desperate attempts to stop the weather.

**About this game**
STORMZILLA is a 3D destruction roguelite where you don't fight the storm — you ARE the storm.

- **Grow from F0 to F6.** Everything smaller than you gets sucked in. Everything bigger shows up red — come back when you're hungrier.
- **Fling the junk you've swallowed.** Cows, buses and entire water towers orbit your funnel as ammo. Hurl them at tanks.
- **Build a different storm every run.** 20 upgrades and 6 one-of-a-kind mutations: FIRENADO, SHARKNADO, SNOWNADO, SUPERCELL, IRON MAIDEN, CASHNADO.
- **The military escalates.** Police → National Guard → tanks → jets and freeze drones → bombers → Project AEGIS, a nine-billion-dollar weather-killing walker.
- **Breaking news.** A live satirical news ticker reports on your crimes against meteorology.
- **Permanent progression.** Spend Chaos Points on meta upgrades, unlock new storms and chase 16 achievements.
